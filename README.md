I've worked on this problem from three sides: as an accountant reading the cost, as an SAP architect configuring the logic, and as a product manager rebuilding it as SaaS. Now I'm adding the fourth: data and machine learning.

## Principles

*What I'd bring to a planning product team.*

- **Trust before accuracy →** A forecast planners override every week isn't a forecast. Explainability matters as much as the error rate.
- **Fix the parameters, not the engine →** Most MRP problems come from stale lead times and safety stock, not from the logic itself.
- **Change a decision, not a dashboard →** An AI feature earns its place when it changes a purchase order or a reorder point.
